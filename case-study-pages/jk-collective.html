JK Collective Inc. Company Website

UX Design & Front-End Development • Independent Business Project

Project Overview

JK Collective Inc. is a web and software development company I co-founded. This project is the company's official website — I designed it in Figma and built it as a fully responsive site using React and Vite. The site introduces the company, showcases its services and process, and invites potential clients to start a project. The design leans into a dark, elegant aesthetic with gold accents and refined serif typography to convey quality and craftsmanship.

Design Direction

The brand needed to feel premium, trustworthy, and design-forward — reflecting a company that builds high-quality digital products. I chose a dark background with warm gold accents and an elegant serif typeface, paired with generous spacing and subtle motion. The hero statement, "We turn ideas into products," sets a confident, benefit-focused tone that carries through the whole page.

The Website

I designed and built a single, scannable landing experience that guides visitors from the value proposition through services and process to a clear call to collaborate.

Page Sections
  • Hero: "We turn ideas into products," with clear "Start a Project" and "Our Services" calls to action.
  • Focused on Quality, Delivering Value: Key value points — 100% custom code, full-stack development, fast delivery, and custom-built solutions.
  • What We Can Do For You: Services including Website Design & Development, Web App Development, E-Commerce, UI/UX Design, API Development & Integration, and Maintenance & Support.
  • From Brief to Launch: A clear four-step process — Discovery, Proposal & Quote, Design & Build, and Launch & Handoff.
  • Ready to Start Collaborating?: A detailed contact form to kick off a new project.

Development

After designing the site in Figma, I developed it as a fully responsive website with React and Vite, translating the designs into clean, reusable components. The layout adapts smoothly across desktop, tablet, and mobile, and the site is deployed and live at jkcollective.ca.

Final Solution & Design Brief

The final result is a polished, responsive company website that positions JK Collective as a quality-focused development partner and makes it easy for prospective clients to understand the offering and get in touch.

Core Focus Areas

Premium Brand Identity, Clear Service Storytelling, and Easy Client Conversion

Premium Brand Identity

Goal: Convey quality and craftsmanship.

Solution: A dark, elegant theme with gold accents and serif typography.

Impact: A confident, trustworthy first impression.

Clear Service Storytelling

Goal: Help visitors quickly grasp what the company offers.

Solution: Structured services and a transparent, step-by-step process.

Impact: Visitors understand the value and how to work together.

Easy Client Conversion

Goal: Turn interest into inquiries.

Solution: Prominent calls to action and a clear contact form.

Impact: A smooth path from browsing to starting a project.

Design Principles Applied

  • Visual Hierarchy: A benefit-led hero and clear sections guiding visitors toward contact
  • Consistency: A cohesive dark-and-gold system across every section and component
  • Responsive Design: Fluid layouts built in React that adapt across all devices
  • Clarity: Concise copy and a transparent process that build trust
