College Website

UX Design • Team Project (2 members)

Project Overview

This project designed a website for a new post-secondary college in Mauritius — the Business and Information Technology College (BITC) — created in response to the Ministry of Education's initiative to build an institution focused on Business and IT alongside sustainability. The website helps prospective students browse programs and courses and apply, while also letting qualified individuals search and apply for faculty jobs. The design weaves in Mauritius' local culture, history, and natural beauty to attract both local and international students.

Duration

2 weeks

Team

Kate Yang, Matt Robinson

My Role

Logo Design & High-Fidelity Prototype

Tools

Adobe Illustrator, Figma

Empathize

Research

Matt led the research, exploring how the college's identity could be rooted in the spirit of Mauritius. Rather than a generic institution, the design integrates the island's topology, history, art, culture, and cuisine to create a distinctive, welcoming environment for local and international students.

Local Identity to Integrate
  • Local Topology: Rolling hills and coastlines inspiring open, nature-connected spaces.
  • History & Heritage: Sites like Aapravasi Ghat and Le Morne Brabant reflecting the island's rich past.
  • Artistic Influences: Vibrant colors and patterns drawn from Mauritian art and Sega culture.
  • Culture & Religion: An inclusive environment respecting the island's diverse faiths.
  • Tourism & Cuisine: Beaches and local culinary traditions to attract culinary and international students.

Target Market

The website targets students seeking post-secondary education in Business and Information Technology, as well as qualified individuals seeking to work on faculty. For students, it presents programs and courses by category, start date, campus, and availability; for job seekers, it offers a dedicated careers page to support their search.

Define

Content Plan & Structure

We planned the site structure across the key pages a prospective student or applicant would need, with a consistent header, navigation, and footer throughout.

Home Page

Header, navigation, and search; news, events, and announcements; quick links to apply and learn about the college.

Programs & Courses

Full-time, part-time, online, and continuing education programs, with student and teacher testimonial videos.

Login & Careers

Separate student and employee login, plus a careers page for applying to faculty positions.

Technical Standards

To keep the identity cohesive, we defined the visual standards early: a serif typeface (Times New Roman) for an academic, established feel, and a black, white, and red color palette carried across the logo, navigation, and pages.

Ideate

Logo Design

I designed the college's logo — a circular emblem for the "Business and Information Technology College of Mauritius," marked "EST. 2024." It features palm trees on a small island set against a red sun, evoking the island's tropical landscape. The black, white, and red palette establishes the brand identity used throughout the website, tying the institution directly to Mauritius' natural beauty.

Prototype

High-Fidelity Prototypes

Building on the research, content plan, and wireframes, I designed the high-fidelity mockups of the website's core pages — Home, Login, Programs & Courses, Careers, and Full-time Programs. The mockups apply the black-white-red palette and serif typography, with a clear header, navigation, and footer, testimonial sections, and imagery that reflects Mauritius' natural beauty and culture.

Final Solution & Design Brief

The final solution is a cohesive college website that presents programs, admissions, and careers information while celebrating the identity of Mauritius. A strong visual brand — anchored by the logo and a consistent black-white-red palette — ties the pages together into a welcoming, credible institution.

Core Focus Areas

Distinctive Brand Identity, Clear Program Discovery, and Local Cultural Integration

Distinctive Brand Identity

Problem: A new college needs a memorable, credible identity.

Solution: A custom logo and consistent color and type system across every page.

Impact: A recognizable brand that stands out to prospective students.

Clear Program Discovery

Problem: Students need to find and apply to the right programs easily.

Solution: Programs organized by type, with search, testimonials, and clear apply paths.

Impact: Faster, more confident decisions for applicants.

Local Cultural Integration

Problem: A generic site wouldn't attract local or international students.

Solution: Imagery, colors, and content rooted in Mauritius' landscape and heritage.

Impact: An authentic, inviting experience that reflects the island.

Design Principles Applied

  • Visual Identity: A logo and brand system rooted in Mauritius' natural and cultural symbols
  • Consistency: Uniform header, navigation, footer, typography, and color across all pages
  • Visual Hierarchy: Clear structure guiding users from news and programs to applying
  • Cultural Sensitivity: Inclusive, locally grounded design that respects the island's diversity

Reflect

What I Learned

  • Designing a logo and brand identity grounded in a place's culture and history
  • Carrying a visual system consistently from logo into high-fidelity page mockups
  • Collaborating to turn research and a content plan into a cohesive website design

Next Steps

If we were to continue this project, we would focus on:

  • Building out responsive layouts for tablet and mobile
  • Adding the remaining pages and testimonial video content
  • Testing navigation and program discovery with prospective students
