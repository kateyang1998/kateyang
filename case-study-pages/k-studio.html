K Studio Mobile App

UX Design • Independent Project

Project Overview

K Studio is a fashion clothing brand, and this project focused on designing a mobile shopping app that lets users browse the store's collections and complete purchases directly in the app. The goal was to create a clean, image-forward shopping experience that reflects the brand's modern aesthetic while keeping browsing and checkout effortless.

Duration

1 week

Team

Independent Project

My Role

UX Researcher, UI/UX Designer, Prototype Designer (end-to-end)

Tools

Figma

Empathize

Competitive Analysis

As an independent project without survey or interview data, I grounded the design in a competitive analysis of two leading fashion e-commerce apps — SSENSE and Zara. Studying how these established brands approach mobile shopping helped me identify best practices and opportunities for K Studio.

SSENSE vs. Zara
Visual Design

SSENSE → minimal, editorial layout with generous whitespace and large product imagery.

Zara → bold, full-bleed photography with a stripped-back, high-contrast interface.

Navigation

SSENSE → clear category hierarchy that makes filtering and finding items easy.

Zara → streamlined catalog with quick access to new arrivals and collections.

Product Browsing

SSENSE → detailed product pages with rich imagery and clear information.

Zara → fast, swipeable galleries that keep the focus on the clothing.

Checkout

SSENSE → structured, step-by-step flow with clear order summaries.

Zara → quick add-to-cart and a short, low-friction checkout.

Key Takeaways for K Studio
  • Lead with large, high-quality product imagery so the clothing stands out.
  • Keep the interface minimal so attention stays on the products.
  • Offer clear, simple category navigation for effortless browsing.
  • Streamline the path from browsing to a low-friction checkout.

Define

Target User

K Studio targets style-conscious shoppers, primarily adults aged 18–45, who prefer to browse and shop for fashion on their phones. These users value a clean, easy-to-navigate experience, high-quality product visuals, and a quick, hassle-free purchase.

Problem Analysis

Drawing on the competitive analysis, I identified common pain points that fashion shopping apps often fail to address well.

1. Cluttered Browsing

Many shopping apps overwhelm users with dense layouts, making it hard to focus on the products themselves.

2. High-Friction Checkout

Long, multi-step checkout flows create frustration and lead to abandoned carts.

3. Weak Brand Identity

Generic, template-like interfaces fail to reflect a brand's aesthetic and feel impersonal.

Problem Statement

Fashion shoppers want to browse and purchase clothing on mobile quickly and enjoyably, but many shopping apps feel cluttered and impersonal, with friction-filled checkout flows. K Studio needs a clean, image-forward app that makes discovering and buying clothing effortless while reflecting the brand's modern aesthetic.

Ideate

Because this was a focused, independent project, I moved directly from research and problem definition into high-fidelity prototyping in Figma. Rather than producing separate low-fidelity sketches or wireframes, I iterated on layout, visual style, and the shopping flow directly within the high-fidelity designs.

Prototype

High-Fidelity Prototypes

I designed interactive high-fidelity prototypes in Figma covering the core shopping flow — from browsing collections and viewing product details to completing a purchase. The design emphasizes large product imagery, a minimal interface, and a streamlined checkout that reflects K Studio's modern brand aesthetic.

Final Solution & Design Brief

The final solution is a clean, image-forward shopping app that lets users browse K Studio's collections and buy items with minimal friction. The design keeps the focus on the products while making navigation and checkout simple and intuitive.

Core Focus Areas

Effortless Browsing, Clear Product Details, and Simple Checkout

Effortless Browsing

Problem: Cluttered layouts make it hard to focus on products.

Solution: A minimal interface with large imagery and clear category navigation.

Impact: Users can discover items quickly and enjoyably.

Clear Product Details

Problem: Users need confidence in what they are buying.

Solution: Detailed product pages with rich imagery, sizing, and clear pricing.

Impact: Builds trust and supports informed purchase decisions.

Streamlined Checkout

Problem: Long checkout flows cause cart abandonment.

Solution: A short, low-friction checkout with clear order summaries.

Impact: Reduces drop-off and speeds up purchases.

Design Principles Applied

  • Visual Hierarchy: Large imagery and clear typography guide attention to the products
  • Law of Proximity: Grouped related information for a clear, scannable layout
  • Consistency: Consistent icons, spacing, and components throughout the app
  • Aesthetic-Usability Effect: A clean, modern look that reinforces the brand and feels easy to use

Reflect

What I Learned

  • How to translate competitive research into concrete design decisions
  • The value of a minimal, image-forward layout in fashion e-commerce
  • Designing and prototyping an end-to-end shopping flow independently in Figma

Next Steps

If I were to continue this project, I would focus on:

  • Conducting usability tests with target users to validate the shopping flow
  • Adding features such as wishlists, filtering, and size guides
  • Exploring personalization and product recommendations
