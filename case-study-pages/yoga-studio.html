Serenity Yoga Studio Website

UX Design & Front-End Development • Independent Project

Project Overview

Serenity Yoga Studio is a landing page I designed and built for a fictional yoga studio. I created the design in Figma and implemented it as a fully responsive, accessible web page using HTML and CSS. The page invites visitors to explore classes and instructors, learn about the studio, read student testimonials, and book a class — all wrapped in a calm, nature-inspired visual style that reflects the studio's brand.

Page Design

I designed the landing page in Figma with a calming, nature-inspired aesthetic — soft greens, generous spacing, and clean typography — to convey a sense of serenity that matches the studio's brand. The page is organized into clear, scannable sections that guide visitors from a welcoming hero straight toward booking a class.

Page Sections
  • Hero: A welcoming headline with a clear "Book a Class" call to action.
  • Featured Classes & Instructors: Hatha, Vinyasa Flow, Restorative, and Power Yoga, each with its instructor.
  • About Our Studio: A short introduction to the studio and its values.
  • What Our Students Say: Testimonials that build trust with new visitors.
  • Join Us Today: Class hours, address, and contact details with a final call to action.

Responsive Design

I implemented the page to adapt seamlessly across three breakpoints — desktop, tablet, and mobile. Multi-column layouts on desktop reflow into stacked, touch-friendly sections on smaller screens, keeping the content readable and the booking action easy to reach on any device.

Accessibility

Accessibility was a core part of this project. I tested the page with the WAVE evaluation tool, which initially flagged six color-contrast errors — the instructor text (#6B9080) in the Featured Classes & Instructors section, and the white text over the light background in the Join Us section.

Fixes Applied
  • Darkened the instructor text color to #406451 for sufficient contrast.
  • Changed the Join Us section background to #406451 — preserving the design's aesthetic while fixing contrast, rather than just recoloring the text.
  • Added email and phone contact details in a highlighted container to make them more prominent.

After these changes, WAVE detected no remaining errors, and the page meets accessibility contrast standards.

Final Solution & Design Brief

The final result is a responsive, accessible landing page — designed in Figma and built with HTML and CSS — that presents Serenity Yoga Studio in a calm, welcoming way and makes booking a class effortless on any device.
