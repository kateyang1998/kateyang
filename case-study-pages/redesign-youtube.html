Re-design YouTube Music Mobile App

UX Design • Team Project (4 members)

Project Overview

This project involved redesigning the YouTube Music mobile app to improve user experience based on comprehensive research and usability testing. Working with a team of 4 members, we conducted quantitative and qualitative research to identify pain points and created a more intuitive interface.

Problem Analysis

Based on our research, we identified three main problem areas:

1. Navigation Issues

Users struggle to find basic features due to poor information architecture

2. Playlist Management

Creating and editing playlists requires too many steps

3. Music Discovery

Recommendation algorithms don't match user preferences effectively

Problem Statement

How might we redesign YouTube Music to provide a more intuitive navigation experience and streamlined playlist management for young music enthusiasts?

Final Solution & Design Brief

Our final design addresses the core problems through three key improvements:

Simplified Navigation

Redesigned bottom navigation with clear icons and labels, reducing cognitive load

Streamlined Playlist Creation

Reduced playlist creation from 7 steps to 3 steps with intuitive drag-and-drop functionality

Enhanced Music Discovery

Improved recommendation engine interface with clear categories and personalized suggestions

Reflect

What I Learned

  • The importance of conducting multiple rounds of usability testing with the same users to track improvement
  • How collaborative design processes can lead to more innovative solutions
  • The value of quantitative and qualitative research in informing design decisions

Next Steps

If I were to continue this project, I would focus on:

  • Testing the design with a larger, more diverse user group
  • Exploring accessibility improvements for users with disabilities
  • Investigating how the design performs across different device sizes
